Internship & Job Hunter Full Pipeline

Target Users: College Students (MSOT) Core Focus: Remote, Flexible, Part-Time Internships Artifact: MVP Scope & Low-Fidelity Wireframes

1. One-Line MVP Scope Statement

Scope Statement: “My MVP lets college students discover and track verified remote, part-time internships matching their class schedules so that they gain career experience without compromising their studies. It will not include employer job-posting accounts, in-app recruiter messaging, or automated video interview prep.”

2. User Journey (6 Steps)

1
Trigger & Problem Recognition
Tanmay (MSOT student) has morning classes until 1 PM. He searches for a graphic design/marketing internship, but standard portals only show full-time, offline roles requiring 40 hrs/week that clash with college.
2
Onboarding & Availability Setup
He signs up and sets non-negotiable boundaries: Remote Only, Max 15 hrs/week, and blocks out his morning lecture schedule on an interactive availability calendar.
3
Curated & Filtered Discovery
The system displays a curated feed of strictly verified part-time, remote opportunities with upfront hourly commitments matching his free afternoon blocks.
4
Schedule & Role Verification
He inspects a design role card and reviews the Schedule Compatibility Breakdown, verifying asynchronous daily deliverables with zero mandatory offline hours.
5
Tailored Application
He clicks apply with a tailored cover snippet highlighting his Figma portfolio and specific weekly afternoon availability.
6
Pipeline Tracking & Follow-up
The role is automatically saved to his Kanban tracker in the Applied column, with an automated 5-day follow-up reminder, eliminating the silence and anxiety of ghosting.

3. Feature Brainstorm (18 Features) & MoSCoW Prioritization

Must-Have (Core MVP — 4 Features Linked to Evidence)

Feature Description Direct Evidence from Interview (Tanmay)
1. Strict Remote & Part-Time Filter Hard filter that completely hides 40h/week full-time and offline roles. “They were full time and they expected me to work with them in offline mode and not work from home... It should be part time.”
2. College Schedule Matcher Students input lecture hours so roles requiring conflicting hours are excluded or flagged. “The hardest part was to find an internship role that has my interest and can go parallel with my college.”
3. Upfront Time-Commitment Card Displays weekly hours, daily time expectations, and stipend clearly on the outer card before clicking. “If I could wave a magic wand I would wish for our internship role that has flexible workers.”
4. Basic Application Tracker (CRM) Simple pipeline tracking application statuses from submission to follow-up. “...across that whole journey from finding the role to applying to waiting for feedback...”

Should-Have (v2 — 5 Features)

  • 5. Automated Follow-Up Reminders: Alerts after 5-7 days of silence.
  • 6. In-Browser Form Auto-Filler: Extension for common form fields.
  • 7. AI Resume Match Score: Percentage fit for portfolio vs JD.
  • 8. Minimum Stipend Filter: Filter out unpaid internships.
  • 9. Direct Email Generator: 3-sentence outreach note to hiring leads.

Could-Have (Nice Extras — 5 Features)

  • 10. Telegram/WhatsApp Alerts: Ping when a high-match role appears.
  • 11. Exam Blackout Mode: Pauses outreach during exam weeks.
  • 12. Portfolio Preview Tile: Embedded Behance/Figma link preview.
  • 13. Drag-and-Drop Kanban: Interactive status board.
  • 14. Interview Prep Sheet: 5 role-specific prep questions.

Won’t-Have Now (Parked on Purpose — 4 Features)

  • 15. Employer Job-Posting Portal: Aggregating public listings first.
  • 16. AI Mock Video Interviews: Out of scope for early validation.
  • 17. In-App Direct Recruiter Chat: Redirect to standard email/portal.
  • 18. Stipend Negotiation Simulator: Not needed for entry internships.

6. Notes from 2 Wireframe Usability Checks

User Check 1: Tanmay (Marketing & Graphic Design Student)
Task Given: "Find a graphic design role requiring under 15 hours a week and save it to your tracking list."
Where they got confused: The weekly hourly requirement was originally hidden inside the expanded job details panel, requiring the user to click into every card individually to check for schedule conflicts.
Change Made: Added an immediate 10–12 hrs/week · Flexible Hours badge directly on the front-facing card in the discovery feed.
User Check 2: Honeyvilla (Peer Student)
Task Given: "Click 'Apply' on a role and check where the application is stored."
Where they got confused: After hitting the 'Apply' button, the screen stayed on the job detail page without any confirmation, leaving the user uncertain whether the application succeeded.
Change Made: Added an instant confirmation toast banner and redirected the status badge to Applied: Follow-up in 5 days on the Kanban board.
