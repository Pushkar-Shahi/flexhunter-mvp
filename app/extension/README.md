# FlexHunter Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)
**Smart Student Internship Autofill & Availability Protection**

This directory contains the unpacked Google Chrome extension for **FlexHunter**.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome

1. Open **Google Chrome** and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
2. Toggle on **Developer mode** in the top right corner.
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top left corner.
4. Select this directory:
   ```
   /Users/shahi/Desktop/Learn/Product Development /app/extension
   ```
5. Pin **FlexHunter** to your Chrome toolbar!

---

## ⚡ What it Does

1. **Job Detection**: Automatically detects job title, company name, and stipend when viewing listings on **Internshala**, **LinkedIn**, **Indeed**, and **Wellfound**.
2. **Availability Safeguard**: Calculates student fit score against your college schedule and 15h/week workload limits.
3. **1-Click Autofill**: Automatically fills your Name, College, Email, and AI-tailored pitch into job portal application inputs.
4. **Instant CRM Logging**: Logs the applied role directly into your FlexHunter Kanban CRM.
