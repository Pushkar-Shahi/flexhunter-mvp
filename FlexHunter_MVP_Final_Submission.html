2. Product Brief & User Journey (6 Steps)

One-Line Scope Statement: “My MVP lets college students discover and track verified remote, part-time internships matching their class schedules so that they gain career experience without compromising their studies. It will not include employer job-posting accounts, in-app recruiter messaging, or automated video interview prep.”
1
Trigger & Problem Recognition

Tanmay (MSOT student) has morning classes until 1 PM. Portals only show full-time offline roles (40 hrs/wk) that clash with lectures.

2
Onboarding & Availability Setup

He sets non-negotiables: Remote Only, Max 15 hrs/wk, and blocks out morning lectures on an interactive 8 AM–7 PM timetable.

3
Curated & Filtered Discovery

The system displays curated part-time, remote opportunities with upfront commitments matching his free afternoon blocks (68 free hrs/wk).

4
Schedule & Role Verification

He inspects a design card and reviews the AI Schedule Compatibility Breakdown, verifying asynchronous deliverables with zero morning clashes.

5
Tailored 1-Click Application

Using the simulated browser extension, he autofills the application with an AI cover pitch highlighting his afternoon bandwidth and Figma portfolio.

6
Pipeline Tracking & Follow-Up

The role logs into his Kanban board in Applied, with an automated 5-day follow-up reminder eliminating recruiter ghosting.
